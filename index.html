Why now

Germany's BESS pipeline is growing faster than any team can coordinate it by hand.

18,158grid-connection requests for battery storage in 2025, up 194% year on year
573 GW vs ~54 GWof storage capacity requested, against what was actually granted
1 Apr 2026the Reifegradverfahren made documentation quality a hard gate for winning capacity

Sources: Bundesnetzagentur monitoring data (via pv magazine, Aug 2026); transmission-operator procedure change, April 2026.

A delayed transformer, a revised permit condition, a supplier's updated quote: each one has to be traced by hand through the schedule, the contract and every person it affects. Whether a project is ready for its next filing is often a judgment call, not a defensible score.

The domino effect

One change. Every consequence, traced.

Pick an event. BESSMIND works out what it touches across schedule, contracts, financing, filings and people.
